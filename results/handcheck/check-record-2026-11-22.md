# Label check of the hand-check sheets, 22 November 2026

Who checked: model reviewers run by Claude at Katrina's request, not Katrina. The plan (section 13) names Katrina's reading as the human pass; this check is a further independent model check, blind to the labels in its first reading, and does not replace it.

Procedure: the three sheets (`hard_tier_all`, `sample_100`, `disagreements`; 218 rows) were split into 19 batches. For each batch a blind reader answered the study's own question for every row without seeing the label, and a separate auditor read the rows with their labels looking for indefensible labels, undecidable rows and construction flaws. Every row where the blind answer differed from the label, or either reader raised a concern, went to three independent adjudicators who saw the row, the label and the concerns; a row is rejected when at least two of three adjudicators reject it (label wrong or undecidable under the question).
